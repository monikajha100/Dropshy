Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Dropshy Zero Investment Business Model

Is it possible to start Dropshipping with zero investment? ×
While you don’t need to buy stock, Dropshy helps you minimize these costs by removing the need for warehouses and staff.
What is Dropshy ?+
It is a way to sell products online without buying them first. You show the product, the customer pays you, then you pay the supplier to send the item.
Can I run this business part-time while working a job?+
Absolutely. Since Dropshy handles the "heavy lifting" (inventory and shipping), you only need 1–2 hours a day to manage ads and customer queries.
Is dropshipping still profitable in 2026?+
Yes, but the strategy has changed. Success now comes from "Branded Dropshipping"—creating a unique store identity rather than just selling random items. Dropshy specializes in helping you build these high-value brands.
Can I sell products globally from any country?? +
Yes! With the Dropshy model, you can live in India and sell to the USA, UK, or Europe without ever leaving your home.
Can I start this business part-time ? +
Absolutely. Since Dropshy handles inventory, packaging, and shipping, most of our users spend only 1–2 hours a day managing their ads and customer inquiries.
Is it really "Zero Inventory"?+
Yes! You never have to buy or store any stock. You only buy a product after a customer has already paid you for it.
How do I make money?+
You sell a product for a higher price than what the supplier charges. Example: You sell a watch for $50. The supplier charges $20. You keep $30 as profit!
